According to Paul Graham, there will be "Writes" and "Write Nots." I choose to write.

This blog is a public notebook. I write about Cloud, building tools, open source, Data Analytics, Databases, and occasionally about good books and music. Writing is thinking with clarity — and I want to rekindle that practice.

All content here is human-written. AI helps me build the tools, not the words.
